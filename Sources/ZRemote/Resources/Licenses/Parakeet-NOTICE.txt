ZRemote downloads the pinned Core ML model files without modifying them.
Fluid Inference converted the original NVIDIA models to Core ML and quantized
the encoder. ZRemote uses the int8 encoder with the v2 English or v3 multilingual
decoder and vocabulary. Model weights are optional downloads, not bundled assets.
No endorsement by NVIDIA or Fluid Inference is implied.
