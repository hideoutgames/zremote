SwiftDiffs is a native adaptation of @pierre/diffs 1.4.2.
Copyright 2025 Pierre Computer Company and contributors.
Upstream source: https://github.com/pierrecomputer/pierre/tree/main/packages/diffs

ZRemote modifications: bounded parsing; native mobile row models; binary and
incomplete patch states; quoted Git path decoding; removal of AppKit/editor,
syntax highlighting and diff-generation dependencies. ZRemote renders host
patches in a read-only SwiftUI/Skip native component.

No Shiki engine, grammar, theme, jsdiff frontier, or QuickJS component from
Swift-Diffs is incorporated in this adaptation.
